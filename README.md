# Furniture Sales Forecasting using Time Series Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
Accurate sales forecasting is a critical component of effective business planning in the retail industry.  
This project focuses on forecasting **furniture sales** using historical retail transaction data and **time series analysis techniques**.

The objective is to analyze historical sales patterns, identify trends and seasonality, and build forecasting models that can predict future sales to support **inventory planning, demand forecasting, and strategic decision-making**.

This project demonstrates an **end-to-end data science workflow**, from raw data preprocessing to actionable forecasting insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
Retail businesses frequently face challenges such as:
- Overstocking and understocking of products
- Inefficient inventory management
- Revenue loss due to inaccurate demand predictions
- Poor supply chain and procurement planning

Furniture sales often show **seasonal patterns and long-term trends**, making time series forecasting a valuable solution for anticipating future demand and optimizing operations.

---

## 🧠 Project Objectives
The key objectives of this project are:
- Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) on furniture sales data
- Analyze time-based trends, seasonality, and sales patterns
- Prepare and aggregate data for time series modeling
- Build and evaluate forecasting models
- Predict future furniture sales
- Present insights that can help business stakeholders make data-driven decisions

---

## 📂 Dataset Information
- **Dataset Name:** Sample Superstore Dataset
- **Category Analyzed:** Furniture
- **Key Features Used:**
  - Order Date
  - Sales
  - Product Category
  - Sub-Category

The data was aggregated at a time-based level to enable effective time series analysis.

---

## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
The EDA process included:
- Checking missing values and data consistency
- Filtering furniture category data
- Aggregating sales by date
- Visualizing sales trends over time
- Identifying seasonality and fluctuations

These steps helped in understanding the structure and behavior of furniture sales across time.

## 📈 Time Series Analysis & Modeling
The following techniques were applied:

The model was trained on historical data and used to generate future sales forecasts.
